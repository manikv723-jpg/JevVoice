SayOpen: say an app, it opens.

1. Drag SayOpen into Applications.
2. Open it. macOS will say it can't check the developer. Click Done.
3. Go to System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down, click "Open Anyway" next to SayOpen.
   (Only needed once. It happens because this build isn't notarised by Apple.)
4. A waveform icon appears in the menu bar. Click it, paste your TypeSafe API key
   (get one at console.typesafe.ai). The key is saved privately on your Mac.
5. Allow the microphone and speech recognition when asked.

Then just say "open Chrome", "open the app I edit videos in", "notes kholo".
Control + Option + Space turns listening on and off.

Speech is recognised on your Mac. The only thing sent over the network is the
transcript, to api.typesafe.ai, to decide which app you meant.
